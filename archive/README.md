# Liac history

The `legacy-m5` Git tag marks the original Bolt/Fast and Luna/Deep system at
`7510227dcb21212c607532150b864ff7757476ce`. Its code, tests, launchers and
milestone reports are available at that revision. The
[legacy documentation](https://github.com/softsock/liac-ai/tree/7510227dcb21212c607532150b864ff7757476ce/docs)
covers M0 through M5.0, including the Desktop Inspector and Conversation Lab.
Use a checkout of the legacy revision to open its private Lab archives.

Commit `16f8454` records the M6.1 prototype before the Single Mind cutover.
Its tree preserves the transitional blueprint and the original M6.0 plan.
The active [M6 reports](../docs/M6_1_BEA_PERCEPTION_REWRITE.md) cover the
provider and perception experiments. Runtime databases, traces and Lab
archives stay in local storage outside Git.
